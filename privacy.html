1. Scope and contact

This policy describes Logdiabet (iOS bundle ID com.logdiabet.app), provided by the developer/seller identified on its App Store listing, and this legal website. For privacy questions and requests, contact aslantassmehmet1@gmail.com. No Logdiabet account or sign-in is required. This policy explains the current release; it does not grant consent for future collection.

2. Health information stays on your device

The information you enter includes glucose readings, dates and times, meal and time-of-day labels, notes, insulin doses, HbA1c results, weight, diabetes/tracking profile answers, target ranges, and preferences such as language, units and appearance. These records are stored in the app’s local database. We do not receive them on an application server or send them to RevenueCat. The app uses them locally to display your records, charts and settings. A web preview, if used, stores its records in that browser’s local storage instead.

3. Purchases and RevenueCat

We use Apple’s App Store and RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore eligible access, manage Pro entitlements, prevent purchase fraud and understand subscription performance. RevenueCat creates a persistent pseudonymous customer identifier without asking for your name or email. It is not a guarantee of complete anonymity. Purchase and subscription details (such as product, transaction/receipt information, purchase dates, expiry/refund status and entitlement) and technical information needed to operate the service (such as app/OS version, store country/currency, network request and IP information) may be processed by these services. We do not send your health profile, readings, insulin, labs, weight or notes to RevenueCat. Automatic device-identifier collection is disabled in our SDK configuration. We have not enabled advertising attribution or cross-app advertising tracking. Payment card information is handled by the store and is not available to us.

4. Support and legal website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments you choose to send, to answer your request and resolve issues. Please avoid sending medical records, full receipts or payment details unless specifically necessary; redact unrelated information. Our email service provider processes correspondence. The legal website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses, browser/device details, request times and security/service logs when you visit. We have added no analytics, advertising scripts, third-party fonts or tracking cookies to these pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

5. Purposes and legal grounds

Local records are used for the personal tracking functions you choose. Purchase verification and support are used to provide the service or respond to your request. Where applicable, contractual necessity supports service delivery; legal obligations support required transaction records; and legitimate interests support proportionate security, fraud prevention and issue resolution, subject to your rights. Where consent is legally required, including for any future processing of special-category health data outside your device, it must be obtained separately before that processing begins. Merely reading this policy is not consent. We do not use health records for advertising, sell them, or make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

6. Recipients and international processing

Apple and RevenueCat process purchase information; our email provider processes support messages; and GitHub hosts these pages. Providers and their subprocessors may operate in countries other than yours, including the United States, where laws can differ. Applicable transfer safeguards or another valid transfer mechanism are required where the law requires them; this policy is not blanket consent to international transfers. Read the providers’ information: Apple and RevenueCat. We may disclose information we actually hold where legally required or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, with appropriate limits. We cannot disclose local health records that we do not possess.

7. Retention, deletion and backups

Local records have no automatic expiry and normally persist when you close or update the app. Settings → Delete all data resets the app’s local health records and profile. Uninstalling, clearing app/browser data, losing the device or resetting it may erase records. Operating-system/device backups may retain or restore copies under your own backup settings; the app does not provide a cloud health-record backup or recovery service. Local deletion is not a forensic secure-erase guarantee for storage remnants or backups. We cannot remotely restore your records. Purchase records and support correspondence are kept only as needed for service, dispute resolution and applicable legal requirements; provider retention may differ. Deleting local data or uninstalling does not cancel a subscription or automatically delete Apple/RevenueCat transaction data. Contact us for requests concerning information we hold, and manage store/backup data with the relevant provider.

8. Security

We rely on the device’s app sandbox and operating-system protections for local records; the app does not add its own database encryption. Purchase service requests use encrypted network connections. Protect your device with a passcode and keep software up to date. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Do not send private records to the public GitHub repository or its issues.

9. Your choices and rights

You can review your local records, change profile/preferences, and delete local data in the app. For information held by us, contact aslantassmehmet1@gmail.com. Depending on applicable law, including the GDPR or Turkey’s Law No. 6698 where applicable, you may request information/access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, and complain to a competent data protection authority. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing. We may request only proportionate information to verify a request and respond within the applicable legal period. Rights are subject to lawful exceptions. We do not need your medical readings to identify a purchase support request. Your statutory rights are not limited by the absence of a Logdiabet account.

10. Children and caregiver records

The app is not directed to children under 13. Anyone below the age required to consent or contract in their country should use it with a parent or legal guardian and appropriate professional guidance. If you track another person, you must have authority to do so, explain the storage arrangement and protect their information. If a child has sent personal information to our support address without the required authorization, contact us so we can address it.

11. Apple Health and future changes

Apple Health synchronization, scheduled reminders and PDF report generation are not active in the current release. The app does not currently read or write HealthKit data. A future integration would require an updated notice and any necessary separate permissions before access. We will update this page and its effective date when practices change, with additional notice or consent where required.
